How to take this quiz
  • Pick your teacher & subject, enter your full name, and pick your Grade & Section from the dropdown, then tap Start Quiz.
  • You'll need the class access code given by your subject teacher to unlock the quiz — without it, you can't proceed.
  • Each question has a countdown timer shown in the ring at the top right. Watch it — it doesn't pause.
  • Tap an answer choice to select it. Once you tap, your answer locks in immediately — you'll see if it was right or wrong, and you can't change it afterward.
  • If the timer runs out before you select an answer, that question is automatically marked wrong, and the quiz moves on to the next question.
  • You can only take each quiz once, so make sure you're ready before you tap Start Quiz.
